# 75’s Magic Day · 分镜与台词 v1

定稿：4 章，12 页。每章 3 张内页插画、4 条台词，共 16 条台词；封面另计。第 1 页沿用现有插图，以下提示词用于后续制作或补画。本稿不代表网页已经更新。

## 共用画面约定

- **75**：以现有封面和起床图为准。黑发、扎在脑后的两根短辫、圆脸和有表演感的神态保持一致。第 1–4、12 页穿同一套淡紫睡衣；第 5–11 页沿用封面的深靛紫尖帽、短披风、淡紫裙、白袜、红棕皮鞋和单颗金星魔法棒。
- **吱吱**：紫色扁圆身体、白色椭圆肚斑、浅绿脸带、偏斜的大眼、带缝线的黄嘴和两只黄脚。两簇深蓝扇形额羽带浅蓝、米白、紫和珊瑚红纹样。第 1–3 页是不会动的玩偶；第 4 页才眨眼、开口。活过来后仍保持布偶的比例和外形。
- **场景**：卧室沿用首图的木床、花被子和窗帘。第 5、6、11 页是同一扇紫红圆拱门；去程从家往花园看，返程从花园往家看。小兔子第 7 页首次出现，固定奶白毛、垂下一只耳朵、无衣饰。
- **版式**：统一横向 4:3，按单页构图。主体、黄脚、胡萝卜尖等线索离画边至少约 8%；在手机上缩小仍看得懂。画内不放文字、对话框、页码或标志，台词在页面上另排。

生成每页时，先使用下方共用英文前缀，再拼接该页提示词，并附上该页标注的图像参考。参考图只用于指定角色、场景或物件，不照搬上一页的姿势。

```text
Create one original landscape 4:3 interior illustration for the same early-reader picture book as the supplied visual references. Match the existing hand-inked 2D gouache and colored-pencil rendering on warm, uncoated paper, with large readable silhouettes, simple color shapes and sparse texture. Keep 75's distinctive round face, black hair and two very short pigtails tied behind her ears; no buns, long braids or loose long hair. Match Zhizhi's plush design: squat lavender body, white oval belly patch, lime face band, two large slightly tilted white eyes, rounded seamed yellow beak, golden fabric feet, purple wings, and two broad scalloped dark-blue forehead fans with cream/sky-blue and purple/coral markings. No other plush characters. Use a single visual action and preserve comfortable margins around faces and story clues. Do not add text, speech bubbles, captions, numbers, logos, watermarks, panels, glossy 3D rendering or photorealistic skin.
```

## 第一章 · 早上好

### 01 · 起床

**分镜**：晨光照进卧室，75 坐起来伸懒腰，吱吱靠在枕边。

**台词**：75 — “Good morning!”

**画面提示词**（现图可直接使用；补画时以起床图为参考）：

```text
Establish the bedroom in early morning. 75 sits upright in her oak bed in pale lavender pajamas, stretches both arms and smiles. Zhizhi sits inert against the pillow. Show the peach-floral quilt across the foreground and warm daylight from the curtained window. Both the waking gesture and the toy's position beside her should be obvious.
```

### 02 · 空枕头

**分镜**：75 一掀被子，吱吱滚进被子底下。她看着空枕头，被角外只露出吱吱的一只黄脚。

**台词**：75 — “Where are you?”

**画面提示词**（参考第 1 页的卧室、睡衣和花被子）：

```text
Continue immediately in the same morning light and the same bed. 75 has shifted the peach-floral quilt and turns toward the now-empty pillow, curious rather than distressed. Show one small golden fabric foot peeking from a fold of the quilt in the foreground, outside her line of sight. The reader can see the clue at phone size. Keep all the rest of Zhizhi hidden; do not show its eyes, beak or crest.
```

### 03 · 找到吱吱

**分镜**：75 掀开被角，把吱吱抱起来。吱吱还是个安静的玩偶。

**台词**：

- 75 — “There you are!”
- 75 — “Good morning, Zhizhi!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 1 页的玩偶外形、第 2 页的被子位置）：

```text
In the same bed, 75 has turned back and lifted Zhizhi from under the folded quilt. Show her holding the toy close in a small, relieved hug, with Zhizhi's crest and full face readable. It is still an ordinary floppy stuffed toy: fixed embroidered eyes, closed seamed beak, relaxed wings. No wink, sparkles or magic yet.
```

## 第二章 · 魔法门

### 04 · 吱吱回了一声

**分镜**：吱吱在 75 怀里眨了眨眼，回了一声早安。75 低头看它。

**台词**：

- 吱吱 — “Good morning!”
- 75 — “You can talk!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 3 页的抱姿和角色位置）：

```text
Stay in the same bedroom and pajamas; frame a closer view of 75 looking down at the toy she is holding. Zhizhi gives its first tiny wink, one eye still open, with a subtle opening at the beak seam. 75's raised eyebrows and parted lips show a happy startle. Keep the toy unmistakably fabric and make the exchanged glance the only focal action.
```

### 05 · 墙上的门

**分镜**：75 戴上尖帽、披上短披风，举起星星棒。眼前的墙上亮起一扇门。

**台词**：75 — “Look! A magic door!”

**画面提示词**（参考现有封面的魔法装、星星棒和门框，第 1 页的卧室用色）：

```text
Show the moment after 75 has changed into her established wizard outfit. She lifts the single gold-star wand toward a simple interior wall, where the outline of one closed mulberry arched door lights up softly. Zhizhi watches at her feet. Keep a little of the familiar room visible. The door is the main discovery; keep the outfit change offscreen and the garden fully hidden.
```

### 06 · 跨过门槛

**分镜**：75 推开门，门外是一座花园。她抱着吱吱迈过门槛。

**台词**：75 — “Let’s go!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 5 页的门、封面的花园色调）：

```text
View the same arched doorway from inside the house. It now opens onto a mint-green garden and a simple stepping-stone path. In a three-quarter back view, 75 holds Zhizhi securely under one arm and takes one clear step over the sill. The open door and one foot across the threshold should tell the story immediately. No rabbit yet.
```

## 第三章 · 胡萝卜在哪儿

### 07 · 遇见小兔子

**分镜**：小兔子低着头，在花园里翻找。75 蹲下来问它怎么了。

**台词**：

- 75 — “Are you OK?”
- 小兔子 — “I can’t find my carrot.”

**画面提示词**（参考第 6 页的花园和魔法装）：

```text
Beside the garden path, a small cream-white rabbit with one ear folded down looks worried and searches the low leaves. 75 crouches beside it at eye level, with Zhizhi close by. Use the rabbit's searching posture and 75's attentive face to show the exchange. Establish a patch of broad green leaves that will remain in the next two pages. No visible carrot yet.
```

### 08 · 一点橙色

**分镜**：75 把星星棒举到叶子底下，一点橙色从叶片后面露出来。

**台词**：75 — “Look!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 7 页同一块叶丛）：

```text
Move closer to the same leaf patch. 75 lowers the star wand so its small warm glow catches a tiny orange carrot tip partly hidden under one broad leaf. Follow her gaze toward the orange clue. Show only enough orange for a young reader to notice; do not expose the whole carrot. The rabbit and Zhizhi may watch from the side without competing for attention.
```

### 09 · 胡萝卜找到了

**分镜**：75 拨开叶子，把胡萝卜递给小兔子。

**台词**：75 — “Here it is!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 7 页的小兔子和第 8 页的叶丛）：

```text
Show the payoff in the same garden: the leaf is pushed aside and 75 offers one whole orange carrot to the little cream-white rabbit. The carrot is clearly visible between her hand and the rabbit's reaching paws. The rabbit's face brightens. Keep the framing simple and do not add a second carrot, a dance or a new magical effect.
```

## 第四章 · 回家

### 10 · 跳个舞

**分镜**：小兔子抱着胡萝卜高兴地跳起来，75 跟着跳，吱吱也拍起了翅膀。

**台词**：75 — “Let’s dance!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 9 页的角色与胡萝卜）：

```text
In the same clearing, the rabbit makes a small happy hop while holding its carrot. 75 mirrors it with one playful raised heel, and Zhizhi flaps its two soft purple wings close to the ground. Use three readable poses and modest room around them. Keep Zhizhi round and unmistakably a plush toy brought to life; no extra figures or confetti.
```

### 11 · 门边道别

**分镜**：75 抱着吱吱走回魔法门，在门边转身向小兔子挥手。

**台词**：

- 75 — “Bye, Rabbit!”
- 小兔子 — “See you!”

**画面提示词**（参考第 6 页的门；机位移到花园一侧）：

```text
Show the same mulberry arch from the garden side. Warm interior light on the far side signals the way home. At the threshold, 75 holds Zhizhi in one arm and turns back to wave with the other. The little rabbit waves from the garden with the carrot tucked close. Clearly separate the home side and garden side so the return direction cannot be mistaken.
```

### 12 · 晚安

**分镜**：回到开场的卧室。帽子和星星棒放在床边，75 给吱吱也盖上了一点被子。

**台词**：75 — “Good night, Zhizhi.”

**画面提示词**（参考第 1 页的卧室、睡衣和花被子；第 3 页的玩偶）：

```text
Echo the opening composition in the same bedroom, now at bedtime. The window is dusk-blue and a small warm bedside light illuminates the oak bed. 75 is back in her pale lavender pajamas, gently tucking the same peach-floral quilt around Zhizhi beside her. Leave its familiar blue fans and yellow beak visible above the quilt. The wizard hat and star wand rest unlit by the bed. A quiet final picture, with no magic door and no new event.
```

## 出图顺序

先保留第 1 页，补第 2、3 页。先核对同一间卧室、同一床被子、同一只吱吱，再检查两页翻阅时能不能看清「露出黄脚 → 找到吱吱」。第一章通过后，再依次制作后续章节；每章都用上一页的成图校对连续性。
