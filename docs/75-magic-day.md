# 75’s Magic Day

本地运行 `hugo server -D`，从「作品」进入，或直接打开 `/works/75-magic-day/`。作品保留草稿状态。

`data/works/magic_day.json` 保存目录和各章的页数据（插画和英文台词）。全书四章十二页都可阅读，页码跨章节连续。浏览器会在本机记住上次停留的页，读完后可从头再读。无 JavaScript 时十二页依序显示，英文朗读由浏览器提供，没装英语语音仍可阅读。绘本页面及其导航使用英文。

封面和十二张内页插画由 Codex 内置 imagegen 生成，网页资源是 4:3 WebP。标题和台词由页面排版，图中不嵌字。真人照片只用于角色表情参考，原图不进入仓库。全书分镜与台词见 [v1 制作稿](75-magic-day-storyboard-v1.md)，第 4–12 页的实际提示词见[出图记录](75-magic-day-render-prompts.md)。

## 角色参考

吱吱（英文目录写作 Zhizhi）是 75 的专属玩偶。画成扁圆的紫色身体、黄嘴和黄脚、浅绿脸带与两簇深蓝扇形额羽，额羽保留浅蓝、米白、紫色和珊瑚红的纹样。原始玩偶照片只作插画参考。

Create new text-free landscape 4:3 cover art for the original Chinese child's English picture book “75's Magic Day.” Image 1 is a reference for 2D painting medium, lively expression and navy/plum palette only, NOT for its long braids, garments, or composition. Image 2 is a private reference for the child's expression and cheek shape only, NOT for photographic reproduction, clothes, environment or other people. Slightly uneven eyebrows when she performs, animated half-moon smile, black eyes, warm terracotta blush. She raises a single mustard-yellow star wand in a playful stage pose with one heel lifted. Setting: an open mulberry-colored magic door standing just where familiar warm wood floor meets a pale mint garden; a few small leaves beyond the doorway, gold light at threshold. Put the girl and Teddy in front of the door, central and large, clearly readable in a very small thumbnail, with hat, wand and both shoes comfortably inside the image. Hand-inked forms, opaque gouache and sparse colored-pencil scuffs, uncoated cream paper, sharp silhouettes, tactile and playfully imperfect, not polished vector or 3D. One coherent scene with breathing room, avoid scattered confetti, elaborate motifs, dramatic lighting, photographic rendering, recognizable branded costumes, letters, typography, numbers, logos, watermarks.
